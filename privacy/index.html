Louie Home

Privacypolicy.

Louie is an easy-running buddy for iPhone and Apple Watch. It is built to work entirely on your devices.

What it uses

With your permission, Louie reads heart rate, resting heart rate, date of birth, walking and running distance, steps, active energy and workouts from Apple Health, and reads step cadence from your devices' motion sensors. It saves each run to Apple Health as a workout, with its heart rate, distance and active energy.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Your health and motion data stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch and in Apple Health. Louie has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not sell or share your data with anyone.

Your settings and run summaries are stored on your iPhone and are removed when you delete the app. Workouts saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them.

Your control

You can turn off Louie's access to Health at any time in the Settings app › Health › Data Access & Devices › Louie, and turn off motion access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Motion & Fitness.
